About Me

I am a Computer Science student with a growing focus on building technology that is not only functional, but also meaningful to the people who use it. My academic journey has given me the opportunity to explore different areas of computing and understand how software can be used to address practical problems.

My experience spans software development, databases, web technologies, data analytics and artificial intelligence. Through academic projects and independent development, I have learned to approach problems from both a technical and user-focused perspective — from designing a system and structuring its data to developing an interface and testing the final solution.
